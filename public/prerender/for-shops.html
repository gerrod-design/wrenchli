What happens when a customer chooses your shop

  1. Customer describes their symptoms — A vehicle owner in Metro Detroit describes their car problem on Wrenchli.
  2. Your shop appears in their results — If your shop is in our partner network and near their location, your shop appears with your Verified Score.
  3. You receive an alert — You get a notification with the customer's vehicle details, symptoms, top 3 likely causes, and a fair cost range.
  4. The customer arrives prepared — No 20-minute intake conversation. No sticker shock. You can focus on the repair.
  5. Confirm the outcome — This updates your Verified Score and increases how often Wrenchli recommends your shop.

Your Verified Score is yours to earn

Your Verified Score is calculated from three things: how often our assessment matched what you actually found, how your pricing compares to the local market average, and ratings from customers who visited through Wrenchli.

We require a minimum of 5 confirmed outcomes before displaying a score publicly.

No surprises after 90 days

When your 90-day pilot ends, nothing happens automatically. We schedule a call to review your results. There is no auto-billing, no contract renewal, and no pressure.

If you decide to leave at any time: your shop listing is removed within 48 hours. No customer data is retained under your shop profile after you leave.
