Subprocessors

Last updated: April 2026

Wrenchli uses the following third-party services to operate our platform. Each subprocessor is listed with the type of data shared and relevant compliance notes.

Anthropic PBC

Purpose: AI symptom assessment generation. Data sent: Symptom description, vehicle year/make/model. No training use.

Supabase Inc.

Purpose: Database and authentication. Data sent: All user account and session data. SOC 2 Type II certified.

Vercel Inc.

Purpose: Hosting and CDN. Data sent: Request logs. No personal data stored beyond standard logs.

Google AI (Gemini)

Purpose: Audio and video analysis features only. Data sent: Audio/video recordings submitted for analysis. Subject to Google AI terms.

Stripe Inc.

Purpose: Payment processing (Pro subscriptions). Data sent: Payment card data. PCI DSS compliant, Wrenchli never stores card data.

NHTSA

Purpose: Vehicle recall lookup. Data sent: VIN. US government public API, no data retained.

Questions about our subprocessors? Contact us at support@wrenchli.net.
