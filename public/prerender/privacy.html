Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 | Version 1.2

Wrenchli, Inc. | Delaware Corporation | Contact: privacy@wrenchli.net

1. Introduction

Wrenchli, Inc. ("Wrenchli," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit wrenchli.net and use our services — including our vehicle symptom assessment tool, repair shop matching, and repair financing features.

This policy covers two types of users:

  • Consumers — vehicle owners who use Wrenchli to assess symptoms and find repair shops
  • Shop Partners — repair shops that join the Wrenchli partner network

2. Categories of Data Collected

We collect the following data when you use Wrenchli:

  • Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) — used for recall lookup via NHTSA.
  • Vehicle year, make, and model — used to generate your symptom assessment.
  • Symptom descriptions you enter — transmitted to Anthropic (Claude API) for assessment generation.
  • Audio or video recordings if you use the audio feature — transmitted to Google AI (Gemini), not stored by Wrenchli after analysis.
  • Email address if you create an account.
  • Payment information if you subscribe to Pro — processed by Stripe, never stored by Wrenchli.

3. Data Retention Schedule

  • VINs and vehicle data: retained while your account is active, deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Symptom descriptions: retained for 12 months then anonymized.
  • Audio/video: not retained after analysis.
  • Payment records: retained 7 years per financial regulations.

4. VIN and Vehicle Data

Your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is used to decode your vehicle's exact specifications via the NHTSA public API, improve the accuracy of your symptom assessment, and enable recall lookup for your specific vehicle. Your VIN is never included in AI training data. Your VIN is shared with the partner shop you select as part of intake — it is not shared with any other third party except as required by law.

5. How Repair Outcome Data Flows

When you complete a symptom assessment and visit a partner shop, your name, contact information, vehicle details (including VIN if provided), symptom description, and assessment summary are sent to the shop you select. When the shop closes your repair order, they may confirm what they found, what they charged, and what parts were used. This closes your outcome loop, contributes to the shop's Verified score, and (in anonymized form) improves Wrenchli's AI system.

6. Repair Financing

If you use Wrenchli's repair financing feature, your name, contact information, vehicle information, and symptom assessment summary will be shared with our lending partner(s) as part of the loan application process, with your explicit consent. Applying for financing is always optional.

7. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services receive data in connection with Wrenchli's operation: Supabase (database/auth), Anthropic Claude API (AI assessment), Google AI Gemini (audio/video analysis), Stripe (payments), NHTSA (VIN decode), shop management systems (Tekmetric/AutoLeap/Mitchell 1), and analytics providers.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise these rights, email privacy@wrenchli.net.

Michigan Residents (MCDPA)

If we deny your privacy request, you have the right to appeal by emailing privacy@wrenchli.net with subject line MCDPA Appeal. We respond to appeals within 60 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest, access controls, and audit logging. Shop management system credentials are stored encrypted and never accessible in plaintext.
