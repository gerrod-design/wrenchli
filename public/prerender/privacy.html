Privacy Policy

How Wrenchli collects, uses, and protects your vehicle and assessment data.

Your Privacy Rights

Michigan and Ohio residents have the following rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the vehicle and symptom data we have collected about you.
  • Right to delete: Request deletion of your vehicle details, symptom descriptions, and assessment history at any time.
  • Right to correct: Request correction of any inaccurate information in your profile.
  • Right to opt out: Opt out of your assessment data being shared with partner shops at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@wrenchli.net. We will respond within 45 days.

We do not sell your personal information, including your VIN, symptom descriptions, or assessment history, to any third party.
