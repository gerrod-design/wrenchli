What Is Wrenchli?

Wrenchli is a vehicle symptom assessment platform that connects vehicle owners with trusted local repair shops. We provide instant symptom assessments, transparent pricing, and repair financing on the way — so nobody has to choose between fixing their car and paying their bills.

Our mission: Mobility for All.

How It Works — For Vehicle Owners

  1. Describe your symptoms — Tell Wrenchli what's happening with your vehicle (noises, warning lights, performance issues).
  2. Get an instant symptom assessment — Wrenchli analyzes symptoms against millions of repair cases and returns likely causes, urgency level, and estimated cost range.
  3. Compare local shops — See transparent pricing from trusted shops near you. No hidden fees.
  4. Book your repair — Schedule the work with a shop you trust. Repair financing options are on the way.

How It Works — For Repair Shops

  • Customer acquisition — Get connected with nearby vehicle owners who need repairs you specialize in.
  • Shop management tools — Scheduling, invoicing, and customer communication ($299/mo).
  • Quality benchmarks — Track performance metrics and build a verified reputation.

Assessment Accuracy

Wrenchli's symptom assessment engine is continuously validated against real-world repair outcomes. We track every assessment through to completion and publish accuracy metrics. Our system identifies the correct root cause or a closely related issue in the majority of cases, and confidence calibration tells you when we're less certain.

All assessment results include urgency ratings, DIY feasibility, and cost estimates benchmarked against local market rates.
