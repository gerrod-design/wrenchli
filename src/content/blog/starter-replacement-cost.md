## Short answer

Most owners pay $395 to $840 to replace a starter, parts and labor together. The starter itself runs $200 to $450, labor runs $195 to $390, and the job usually takes 1.5 to 3 hours at a shop.

## What the starter does

It is the electric motor that cranks the engine when you turn the key or press the button. It works for a few seconds at a time, and it either works or it does not, which is why starter failures feel sudden even when they have been building for weeks.

## Signs yours may be failing

- A single loud click when you turn the key, and the engine does not crank
- Grinding or whirring during a start attempt
- The car starts fine sometimes and not at all other times, with no pattern tied to weather
- Everything electrical works, lights, dash, radio, but the engine will not turn over
- Needing a smack-and-retry or several attempts before it catches (a temporary trick, not a fix)

## Why the price range is wide

Access, again. On some engines the starter sits in the open and swaps in an hour and a half. On others it hides under the intake manifold or behind other components, and the labor doubles. A new starter also costs more than a remanufactured one, and for many vehicles a quality remanufactured unit is the normal, sensible choice. Ask which one a quote is for.

Before you pay for a starter, rule out the battery. A weak battery and a dying starter produce nearly the same click from the driver's seat, and the battery is the cheaper repair by hundreds of dollars. Any shop should test the battery and the charging system before quoting a starter, and a quote that skips that step is a quote to question. Our [guide to a car that clicks but will not start](https://www.wrenchli.net/blog/car-wont-start-clicking-noise) walks through how to tell the difference.

## Is it safe to keep driving?

A failing starter is not a safety problem while the car runs. It is a stranding problem: every start is a coin flip, and the failure does not care whether you are at home or two states away. Once starts become unreliable, schedule the repair on your terms instead of the starter's.
